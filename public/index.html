FAQs

Common questions

Can I learn Bike + License?

Yes. The course starts from zero, with no driving experience needed.

Do you help with the sign test?

Yes. Use the sign test and sign book tools on this page.

Are fees refundable?

No. Fees are non-refundable after admission, as stated in the terms and conditions.

How do I enrol?

Choose a package, fill in the admission form on this page, pay the fee and share your receipt, then start driving on your chosen date.

Which payment methods do you accept?

You can pay by cash, bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa.

Is the license included in the course?

In selected packages. The Car Auto + Manual (Rs 35,000) and LTV (45 lessons) packages include the license, and the Bike + License package includes license guidance. Check each package card for details.

Do you teach women and complete beginners?

Yes. We teach men and women of all ages, and our beginner packages are made for first-time learners, with calm, patient instructors in dual-control cars.
